# Product Brief

## One-line proposition
WORKMATE AI is a personal AI communication coach that helps Sales & Account Managers handle real client conversations more clearly, naturally, and persuasively.

## Problem
Client-facing professionals may know their product and commercial objective but struggle to express themselves in a second language under pressure: price negotiation, objections, proposals, difficult clients, and follow-up.

Generic chatbots can rewrite a sentence but do not reliably build a longitudinal model of a user's recurring communication weaknesses and turn that evidence into targeted practice.

## Initial customer
- Sales Representatives
- Account Managers
- Business Development Professionals
- Customer Success / Client-facing professionals

## Core job to be done
"When I need to communicate with a client in a high-stakes professional situation, help me practice the conversation, understand what I did poorly, and improve the next attempt."

## Differentiation hypothesis
The product's potential advantage is an evidence-backed Communication DNA:
- tracks recurring patterns, not just isolated corrections;
- distinguishes grammar errors from stylistic suggestions;
- links feedback to observed examples;
- selects future practice based on repeated weaknesses and user goals.

This is a hypothesis to validate, not a proven moat.

## Initial experience
1. Onboard user and collect language preferences and professional context.
2. Run a short baseline assessment.
3. Let user select a business scenario and difficulty.
4. Conduct text-based roleplay with a consistent AI counterpart.
5. Evaluate the actual conversation using a defined rubric.
6. Save validated mistakes and update Communication DNA.
7. Recommend a short next exercise.

## Product principles
- Work situations before academic lessons.
- Specific feedback before motivational language.
- Evidence before scores.
- User agency over language preferences.
- Fast sessions and transparent AI usage.
- Privacy by default.

## Validation questions
- Do Sales & Account Managers recognize this as a frequent, costly problem?
- Will they complete a second roleplay within seven days?
- Do they trust and understand the feedback?
- Does personalization make practice feel meaningfully better than a generic chatbot?
- Will they pay for repeated practice?
